Academic Methodology

How the Prediction Works

Step-by-step machine learning workflow designed for retail business sales analytics.

1

Dataset Ingestion

Historical sales data is loaded from the uploaded dataset, verifying order dates, quantities, discounts, and revenues.

2

Data Preprocessing

Transactions are aggregated by month and year. Time indices, calendar months, quarters, and operational averages are created.

3

Model Training

A Linear Regression model is trained using an 80/20 train/test split to fit the underlying secular trend and seasonality patterns.

4

Feature Extraction

When you choose a month from the dropdown, that specific month's feature parameters are retrieved and passed to the model.

5

Model Forecast

The Linear Regression model generates the predicted sales value via the linear coefficients and intercept.

6

Variance Analysis

Actual sales and predicted sales are compared, displaying the exact monetary difference and percentage variance.
